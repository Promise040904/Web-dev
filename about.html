About Golden Crust

A neighbourhood bakery, started in one small oven.

Our story

Golden Crust started in 2018 with one oven and one sourdough starter. We wanted bread that tastes like bread, made without shortcuts or long ingredient lists.

Today we still bake in small batches every morning, and we still use the same starter.

What we care about

  • Simple ingredients: flour, water, salt, yeast, and nothing you can't pronounce.
  • Local suppliers: we buy our flour and eggs from farms close to the bakery.
  • Fair prices: good bread shouldn't be a treat.
